# Impeccable Design System: Luxury Real Estate & Spatial Media Studio

## Domain Persona
**Architectural Digest × Sotheby’s International Realty × High-Performance Creative Suite**
Targeting discerning luxury real estate advisors, architectural buyers, and premier design creators.

## 1. Typography & Hierarchy
- **Primary Editorial Display**: `Playfair Display`, serif. Evokes timeless architectural publication craft, quiet refinement, and editorial dignity.
- **Primary Interface & Prose**: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, sans-serif. Modern, geometric, clean geometric apertures.
- **Tabular Data & Telemetry**: `JetBrains Mono` / `tabular-nums`. For square footage, pricing, GPS coordinates, camera FOV, and video timecodes.

## 2. Color Palette (Editorial Restraint)
- **Canvas Dominant (60%)**: `#ffffff` (Pure Alabaster) & `#0a1317` (Deep Obsidian for 3D and Video Viewports).
- **Structural Surfaces & Hairlines (30%)**: `#f8f9fa` (Subtle Limestone fill), `#e5e7eb` (Subtle Hairline Border).
- **High-Intent Accent (10%)**: `#0052cc` (Refined International Cobalt) & `#1c1e21` (Matte Black Primary Action).
- **Strict Rule**: Zero purple gradients, zero neon radial blur blobs, zero rainbow status badge clusters.

## 3. Zero-Pill & Metadata Discipline
- Metadata items are quiet, unboxed typographic statements separated by middots:
  `Active Private Listing · Bel Air, Los Angeles · 2024 Architectural Build`
- Interactive tabs use clean, tactile segmented containers with subtle active state elevation, avoiding colorful candy tags.
- Section titles lead with tracked uppercase kickers (`text-[11px] font-semibold tracking-[0.2em] text-neutral-500 uppercase`).

## 4. Architectural Photography Priority
- Images are the hero: full-bleed vistas, generous negative space, natural contrast gradients.
- No floating badge clusters obscuring architectural photography.
