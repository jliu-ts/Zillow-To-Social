# Product Truth: Trending Society — Luxury Real Estate & Spatial Media Studio

## Audience & Users
- **Primary Users**: Elite luxury real estate advisors, high-end listing brokers, and architectural marketing directors (representing properties $10M–$100M+).
- **Secondary Users / End Consumers**: Ultra-high-net-worth buyers, architectural enthusiasts, and VIP clients seeking private showings.
- **Operating Context**: Fast-paced media creation for Instagram Reels, TikTok, property presentations, MLS listings, and private buyer pitches.

## Purpose & Mechanism
- **What it makes possible**: Transforms a luxury architectural listing into a complete multi-format spatial media campaign in minutes — spanning real-time 3D walkthroughs, 4K reel video exports, editorial multi-slide carousels, VIP private showing bookings, and white-label agent branding.
- **Key Differentiator**: High-fidelity architectural dignity combined with instant client-side rendering, spatial 3D navigation, zero-friction lead capture, and actual high-definition video export.

## Platform & Stack
- **Platform**: Web (Desktop 1440px primary, responsive mobile/tablet)
- **Framework**: React 18 + Vite + TypeScript (Strict)
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS with CSS variable tokens
- **Components**: shadcn/ui primitives with Phosphor icons
- **3D & Canvas**: HTML5 Canvas, Three.js spatial visualization, MediaRecorder API

## Brand Voice & Principles
- **Tone**: Architectural, editorial, restrained, authoritative, dignified (Architectural Digest × Sotheby's International Realty).
- **Anti-Slop Commitments**: Zero neon gradients, zero generic pill badges, zero fake telemetry badges, zero rainbow statuses. Real content, tactile controls, and high typographic contrast.
